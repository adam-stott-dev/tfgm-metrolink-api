## Source

Data comes from Transport for Greater Manchester's
[GM Public Transport Schedules](https://ckan.publishing.service.gov.uk/dataset/gm-public-transport-schedules-gtfs)
GTFS feed, published under the
[Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).
The feed covers buses and trams; this API keeps only Metrolink.

The timetable is re-imported every night. [`/api/v1/status`](/docs/api/status/get-status)
shows when it was last imported and the dates the feed covers.

## Scheduled, not live

All times are **scheduled**. Planned engineering works are included when TfGM publishes them
in the timetable, but live delays, cancellations and short-notice disruption are not.

## Times and dates

- All times are UK local time (`Europe/London`) and are returned as ISO 8601 with an offset,
  e.g. `2026-10-19T08:04:00+01:00`. Clock changes are handled for you.
- `serviceDate` is the timetable day a tram belongs to. A tram at 00:20 on Saturday
  usually belongs to Friday's service, so its `serviceDate` is Friday.
- A tram's final stop is an arrival, so it is not listed as a departure from that stop.

## Stop IDs

Stops are identified by a slug of their name (`piccadilly`, `deansgate-castlefield`).
Each stop groups its platforms, and you can also look a stop up by any platform's GTFS ID.
Slugs come from the timetable's stop names, so they stay the same unless TfGM renames a stop.
